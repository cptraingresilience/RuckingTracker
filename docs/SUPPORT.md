# Rux support

This page is the support URL submitted to the App Store for **Rux (RuckingTracker)**.

## Get help

- **Bug reports and feature requests:** open an issue at
  https://github.com/cptraingresilience/RuckingTracker/issues
- **Everything else, including privacy and data-deletion requests:** open an issue with the
  `support` label, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile at
  https://github.com/cptraingresilience

We aim to acknowledge new issues within five business days.

## Common questions

**The map is blank or my route is not recording.**
Rux needs location access while the app is in use. Open iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Location Services → Rux** and choose **While Using the App**, then start the ruck again.

**Where are my rucks stored?**
Rucks are saved on your device. If you are signed in, they can also be synced to the Rux
service so they survive a device change. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**How do I delete a ruck?**
Swipe left on the ruck in the Log tab, or open the ruck and tap **Delete Ruck**.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Open an issue as described above, from the account associated with your Rux sign-in. Accounts
and their synced rucks are removed within 30 days of a verified request.

**Can I change units?**
Yes — Settings → Units lets you switch between Imperial and Metric.

## Requirements

- iPhone running iOS 15.0 or later.
- Location access is required only for GPS-tracked rucks; manual logging works without it.
